# Chatify 💬

> A modern, colorful, and fast messaging app built with Flutter.

---

## What is Chatify?

Chatify is a real-time chat application that lets you connect with friends, send messages, share images, and make calls — all from one beautiful app. It works on Android and iOS and automatically adapts to your phone's screen size.

Whether you're catching up with a friend or meeting someone new, Chatify keeps your conversations fast, private, and enjoyable.

---

## Features

### Messaging
- **Real-time 1-to-1 chat** — messages appear instantly without needing to refresh.
- **Image sharing** — send photos directly from your camera roll or camera.
- **Typing indicator** — see when the other person is typing.
- **Read receipts** — know when your messages have been read.
- **Unread badge** — the chat tab shows how many unread conversations you have.

### Friends
- **Find people** — search for other users by name and send them a friend request.
- **Friend requests** — accept or reject incoming requests from one place.
- **Cancel requests** — changed your mind? Cancel a sent request at any time.
- **Remove friends** — manage your friends list and remove connections.
- **Relationship awareness** — the app always shows you the correct action (Add, Requested, Accept, or Friends) based on your current relationship with each person.

### Calls
- **Call history** — view a log of your past audio and video calls.
- **Incoming calls** — receive and answer calls from friends (audio and video).

### Profile
- **Custom profile** — set your display name, a short tagline, and a longer bio.
- **Profile photo** — upload a photo from your gallery or take a new one.
- **Online status** — your friends can see when you're active.
- **Public profile view** — tap any friend's name to view their profile.

### Appearance
- **Dark mode & Light mode** — switch between dark and light themes with a single tap, right from your profile tab. Your preference is saved so it applies every time you open the app.
- **Colorful design** — vibrant purple, coral, and turquoise accents that make the app feel lively without being overwhelming.
- **Consistent look** — every screen, button, and dialog adapts perfectly to whichever theme you choose.

### Account
- **Email sign-up & sign-in** — create an account with just an email and password.
- **Secure sessions** — your session is managed securely. You stay logged in across app restarts.
- **Sign out** — a confirmation dialog prevents accidental sign-outs.

## How to Get Started

1. **Download the app** and open it.
2. **Create an account** — tap "Sign Up", enter your email and a password.
3. **Set up your profile** — add your name, a photo, and a short bio.
4. **Find friends** — go to the Friends tab and search for people you know.
5. **Start chatting** — once someone accepts your request, tap their name to open a conversation.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need an account to use Chatify?**
Yes. An account is required so your messages and friends list are saved and synced across devices.

**Is my data private?**
Chatify only stores what you put in — your name, photo, and messages. Conversations are only visible to the two participants.

**Can I use Chatify on multiple devices?**
Yes. Sign in on any device and your conversations and friends list will be there.

**How do I change my profile photo?**
Go to the Profile tab → tap "Edit Profile" → tap your current photo to pick a new one from your gallery.

**How do I turn on dark mode?**
Open the Profile tab. You will see a "Light Mode / Dark Mode" toggle. Tap it to switch. The app remembers your choice.

**Can I block someone?**
The block feature is available in the codebase and will be exposed in a future update.

**What happens if I lose my internet connection?**
Messages require an active connection. If you go offline, new messages will not load until you reconnect.
